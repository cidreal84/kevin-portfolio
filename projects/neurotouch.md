# NeuroTouch

A computational neuroscience project exploring artificial sensory perception.

NeuroTouch is a software-only research and learning project focused on simulating biological neural activity and exploring how artificial sensory perception could work.

The project starts with biologically inspired neuron models and aims to progressively build more complex neural networks and sensory pathways.

# Current Progress

* Single-neuron Hodgkin–Huxley simulation.
* Biological ion-channel and membrane-voltage modelling.
* Interactive graphical interface.
* Simulation and visualization tools.

# Next Milestone

Milestone 2: Coupled-neuron layer

The next development stage is connecting simulated neurons to explore how neural activity can interact across a network.

# Technology

* Python
* Computational neuroscience
* Hodgkin–Huxley model
* Numerical simulation
* Pytest

# Long-Term Direction

Explore how software-based neural models could represent sensory information and develop artificial sensory perception.
